Features

What you get

Unlimited streaming

Search the YouTube Music catalog, press play, and listen. Live sets and albums are included.

Synced lyrics

Time-synced lyrics from LRCLIB for most tracks. Tap a line to jump to it, or press Sync when you scroll away.

Real music videos

The full player plays the song's actual music video when one exists, and falls back to audio when it doesn't.

Offline downloads

Save tracks to this device and play them without a connection. Progress shows on every track.

Playlists, radio and import

Paste a public Spotify playlist link, build your own playlists, or start a radio from any artist that never stops.

No account

Install and play. There is no sign-up and no tracking. Your library stays on your machine.

Open source

Released under the MIT license. You can read the code, fork it, and build it yourself.

Version 1.6.5

What changed

New in 1.6.5

Trending that never goes blank

The home shelves and Trending view fall back to YouTube Charts when kworb's chart site is unreachable — same keyless pipeline, real playable tracks. A bad chart pass is never cached for hours, a cold server warms the catalog at boot, and a real database (SQLite) now sits under the app. Plus: top-bar navigation like Spotify's, sidebar lyrics that match fullscreen, and taskbar media controls that always respond.

New in 1.6.3

One mark everywhere

The app icon is now the five-bar Resona mark — the same one the app and this site use — in paper cream on a warm-dark tile. Rendered natively at every size so it stays crisp in the taskbar, Dock and start menu.

New in 1.6.2

Control playback from the taskbar

Hover Resona's taskbar icon on Windows and the preview carries Previous / Play / Next — Spotify-style. macOS gets the same three in the dock menu.

New in 1.6.1

Songs start instantly

The stream resolves in the background while your pointer rests on a row, and the next queue track resolves ahead — the first click on a song no longer waits for stream resolution.

New in 1.6.1

Click anywhere to play

The whole track row is clickable now, not just the small play button. The play, like, save and menu buttons keep their own actions.

The app that updates itself

Resona now installs new releases silently at launch — splash-screen progress, no dialogs. This download is the last one you make by hand.

Getting-started checklist

A progress ring by your profile fills as you finish six steps — search, playlist, like, radio, download, import — and each real action ticks itself off.

Real artist artwork

Popular artists on the home screen now show actual YouTube Music photos instead of gradient initials, plus a polished snackbar and centred play buttons.

Questions

Answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Resona is MIT-licensed. It has no accounts, no ads and no paid tier.

Where does the music come from?

From YouTube Music's public catalog. Resona does not host or store any music files.

Does it work offline?

Songs you download inside Resona play offline. Browsing and streaming need a connection.

Can I bring my playlists?

Yes. Paste a public Spotify playlist link into Import. Your library is stored on this device only.

Windows or macOS warned me about the installer. Is it safe?

The warning appears because the installers are not code-signed yet. Verify the SHA-256 checksum above, then on Windows choose More info and Run anyway; on macOS right-click the app and choose Open.
