FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Resona really free? +

Yes — completely. Resona is open source under the MIT license. There are no accounts, no ads and no premium tier. If you ever see a paywall in Resona, you're not in Resona.

Where does the music come from? +

Resona streams from YouTube Music's public catalog through its own lightweight client. You get the same availability as YouTube Music, wrapped in a proper desktop player.

Is this legal? +

Resona is a media interface that streams publicly available content via YouTube Music's public catalog. It does not host, store, or distribute any music files itself. All content remains the property of its respective copyright holders. Users are responsible for complying with the laws and terms of service in their own jurisdiction.

Does it work offline? +

Partially. Songs you download inside Resona play offline. Browsing and streaming new music needs a connection.

Can I import my existing playlists? +

Yes — paste any public Spotify playlist URL into Import, and Resona search-matches every track into a local playlist. Your library is stored on your device only.

Windows flagged the installer — what now? +

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show a warning. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". Verify downloads against the SHA-256 checksum published above and on the GitHub releases page.
